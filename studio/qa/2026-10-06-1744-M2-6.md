## Acceptance criteria (independently verified)
1. Clip shape: VERIFIED. SfxTest passes. My own audit in QaM26Test reads the 16-bit bytes and finds a DC offset of -0.00002 (Select) and -0.00005 (Command). The worst sample-to-sample step stays under the slope of a sine at that pitch and peak, so there are no clicks: 0.1226 vs 0.1402 at 1320 Hz, 0.0614 vs 0.0701 at 660 Hz, 0.0920 vs 0.1051 at 990 Hz. The first and last samples are within 2 LSB of zero, both sides of the Command note boundary are under 0.01, and the zero-crossing counts match the pitch (176 vs ~185, 73 vs ~79, 106 vs ~119; the envelope tails quantize to 0).
2. Selection hooks: VERIFIED. SfxTest passes through the real Match. QaM26 adds more cases. A recall of a group whose units are all dead does not play and does not change the selection. A recall whose live set equals the pruned selection does not play. A minimap left-click (camera jump) plays no Select.
3. Order hooks: VERIFIED. SfxTest passes. QaM26 adds more cases. Five minimap orders in one frame play once. A minimap right-click with nothing selected plays nothing, and with A armed it cancels and plays nothing. A selection freed in the same frame (between input events) gets no Command for a right-click, S, or A, and A does not arm. With 2 of 5 freed, Command plays once and Move goes out to 3 units.
4. Rate limit: VERIFIED. SfxTest uses a hand-driven clock. On the real clock, 300 box selects over 300 frames (2,069 ms) played Select 38 times, under the bound ms/50+1 = 42. 1,000 right-clicks over 200 frames played Command 15 times over 15 frames that actually enqueued something; 940 were dropped because the queue was full with 863 selected.
5. `--mute`: VERIFIED. SfxTest covers the bus state and the counter. QaM26 puts `--mute` before and after each of the 10 other flags, each applied correctly, and puts it after a value-taking flag whose value is missing (`--seed --mute`, which mutes and is not swallowed). `--mute --mute` works; `--MUTE`, `--mute=0` and `mute` are ignored; `--mute true` mid-list leaves the other flags intact. A windowed run with `--units 1000 --zoom 60 --mute --debug-overlay --seed 7 --screenshot` exits 0 with no ERROR.
6. Smoke and existing scenes: VERIFIED (see Baseline). Read-only: `Sfx.cs` and `SfxEvent.cs` contain no `Rts.Sim`, `World` or `Simulation` reference (grep), and the only caller is SelectionController after its existing Enqueue path. So StateHash cannot be affected by sound, and docs/03 says so.
7. Windowed: UNVERIFIABLE for "hears both sounds" (unattended, nobody can listen). Frame time is VERIFIED by screenshot: 2,000 units at zoom 60, tick 0.086 ms, avg 0.125 ms, 119 fps. I took no pre-change baseline at this tier.
8. Docs and scope: VERIFIED. docs/03 "Audio (M2-6)" sits after the M2 Implementation subsections, before "AI architecture". The docs/01 row is present. The diff touches nothing under `sim/**` or `game/data/**`. Code size: 145 + 11 + ~40 lines; the test is 270 lines.
