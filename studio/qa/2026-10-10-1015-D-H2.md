## Findings
- [S4] BUG-0431: Four description wordings get the wrong role, and the bug index still lists BUG-0410 as open. Repro: Sandstorm "Slowed by 30% (cooldown: 12 s)" gives 0 problems (a wrong cooldown is read as the 12 s duration) and "(cooldown: 45 s)" fails. "for 18-second" passes because the bare 18 matches the range. "4 magic dmg a second" passes. "Blinded for 1 second after a unit leaves" fails, and so does "for 12 seconds and its effects linger after it leaves". No shipped text uses any of these wordings.
- Notes (not bugs): "for N s" cannot tell an ability's duration from an applied status's duration, and distance words cover range, radius, sight and reach as one role. Both are already listed as gaps by the developer and the Producer.
