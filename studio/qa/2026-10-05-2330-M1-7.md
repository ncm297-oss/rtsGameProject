## Findings
- [S3] BUG-0055: Own walkers slip past a friendly holding unit plugging a 1-cell corridor. Repro: `QA/OrderQaTests.FriendlyHoldingPlug_InA1CellCorridor_LetsNobodyThrough` (skipped); radius-0.9 holder mid-corridor, 30 own walkers → 10 pass, up to 1.053 m overlap. An enemy holder lets 0 of 30 through. This is the documented soft-clip rule the brief chose, but it contradicts the QA focus's "nobody through". Producer's call.
- [S3] BUG-0054: A command with unknown Flags bits is accepted by `Enqueue` and recorded, and `ReplayFormat.Write` then throws `ArgumentException (InvalidCommand)`, losing the session's replay. Repro: `QA/OrderQaTests.UnknownFlagsEnqueued_RecordedReplay_StillReads` (skipped): `Enqueue(Command.Stop(0, u) with { Flags = 2 })`, then Write. The same already happens for an undefined `CommandKind`.
- [S4] BUG-0056: Nits. `QueuedFlag` is valid on SpawnUnit/Noop. The dev corridor-Hold test doesn't check that walkers stay behind. Unqueued Hold followed by a queued order lasts one tick (by design; a note for the M2-3 HUD).
- Notes (not bugs):
  - The 0.164 m overlap of enemy walkers into a holding plug is identical for a merely stopped plug, so it is pre-existing (cf. BUG-0045).
  - Shoved goal-less units end up to 0.9 m into a holder: the `ShoveSpacing` pack limit, by design.
  - A queued Move to the unit's own cell costs at most 2 ticks over a direct walk (122 vs 120).
  - Unreachable pockets can't be targeted (NavGrid seals them), so "queued leg to an unreachable cell" reduces to the nearest-passable rule.
