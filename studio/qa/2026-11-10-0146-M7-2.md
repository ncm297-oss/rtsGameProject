  - The same per-unit cost feeds the AI's wave `minArmyCost`, reserve, defense and intruder worths.

  - It also covers the docs/03 `minArmyPop` sentence.
  - Latent: no shipped pack.
- Notes (not bugs):
  - (a) Stacking rule: a 0.2 bite on a victim already under a stronger slow (Sandstorm 0.5) extends the 0.5 slow to 40 ticks. Docs/02 and docs/03 document this ("keeps the longer duration and the stronger magnitude"). Sustained dog bites therefore keep a Sandstorm slow on indefinitely, which is worth knowing for the Teblor balance pass.
