## Findings
- [S3] BUG-0077: a building placed in front of a moving column makes the units behind it give up after 20 ticks. Repro: 16 infantry ordered across a 48x24 map; at tick 30, SpawnBuilding a Keep across their path; 8 of 16 end Idle near where they were (control without the building: 16/16 arrive). Cause: `BestRemaining` survives a closing grid change, so the detour counts as no progress. Dev command only today; S2 once construction lands, so it fits M3-2b (closing vs opening changes).
- [S3] BUG-0078: exposure counts a passable neighbour nobody can reach. Repro: five Keeps seal a corridor touching tree A; a worker ordered onto A walks to an unreachable stand cell every 20 ticks for ever, while the reachable tree B is 2 m away (0 wood in 60 s). Dev command only today; M3-3 must refuse sealing placements or make exposure mean "reachable"; docs/03 sentence is stale.
- [S4] BUG-0079: M3-2 nits. A missing `cost` reports 3 errors where `footprint` reports 1. Tests are 1,286 lines against the 1,000-line budget (production 1,309 of 1,500). The docs say `--workers` needs resources, but `--workers 0` with none runs fine.
- Note (not a bug): `buildings.json` lives under `game/data/factions/**`, which the data track owns, but the brief names these files explicitly, and the data rule lets a sim task ship "the minimum entries its own tests need". No data task ran this session.
- Note: a worker that fills up while its player has no drop-off ends its loop Idle with the load (as documented), and a drop-off built later does not re-arm it. A new same-kind Gather delivers the load (QA row). This is consistent with docs/03; the owner may want a "resume" later.
