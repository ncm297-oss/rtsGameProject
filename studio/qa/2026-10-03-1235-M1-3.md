## Findings
- [S3] BUG-0011: Ramp sides are walkable. A unit can step 1.6-3.2 m sideways between a ramp cell and the flat ground beside it (39-58 degrees, against the docs/02 rule that slopes over 30 degrees are impassable). Criterion 3 still holds as written. Producer's call: block ramp sides, or accept it and amend docs/02. Repro: un-skip `QA.MapQaTests.RampSides_NoPassableStepSteeperThan30Degrees`.
- [S3] BUG-0012: `MapGenParams.Validate` accepts `RampWidth`, `RampLength`, `EdgeMargin` or `Level2Inset` = int.MaxValue because its unchecked int arithmetic wraps. `Generate` then throws IndexOutOfRange or ArgumentOutOfRange. `new Heightmap(65536, 65536, empty, empty)` is also accepted. Repro: un-skip `QA.MapQaTests.OverflowParams_ValidateThrowsOrMapIsValid` and `Heightmap_SizeOverflow_IsRejected`.
- [S4] BUG-0013: Params that `Validate` allows can take about 35 s to generate. Example: 512x512, 64+64 plateaus, 16 ramps x 1024 tries, MinPassableFraction 1, MaxAttempts 64. Repro: `Stress.MapStressTests.WorstCaseValidParams_StillBounded` with MaxAttempts 64.
- [S4] BUG-0014: Seed ulong.MaxValue gives exactly seed 0's map. PCG seeding on stream 0 wraps the state to 0, and Lemire rejection swallows the extra leading 0 draw. Repro: un-skip `QA.MapQaTests.SeedMaxValue_AndSeedZero_GiveDifferentMaps`.

Observations, not filed:
- `StateHash` now depends on map params indirectly, through the post-generation MapGen state. This is consistent and deterministic; M1-6 should decide what it wants.
- The silent fallback to a level-missing or flat map gives callers no signal, which is acceptable for now.
- `WorldToCell(-1e-45f)` returns cell 0, because the subnormal divided by 2 rounds to -0. Harmless.
