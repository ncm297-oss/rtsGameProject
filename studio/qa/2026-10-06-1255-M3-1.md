## Findings
- [S3] BUG-0073: A tree falling every tick leaves all but the 2 oldest goal groups without a flow field. One fall per tick for 100 ticks with 32 goal groups: 30 of 32 walkers stand for 90+ ticks, and the average tick goes from 0.014 to 1.82 ms. Latent until M3-2. A single 100-tree burst is fine: longest wait 15 ticks, 0 give-ups, 32 / 32 arrive.
- [S3] BUG-0074: The forest placer assumes 1 x 1 trees. With a 2 x 2 tree footprint (which the loader accepts), 20 of 20 seeds seal pockets and the reported tree count doesn't match the store (238 reported vs 81 live nodes). `TwoByTwoTrees_Report`.
- [S3] BUG-0075: Felling a forest's interior tree first leaves an open cell nobody can reach, which contradicts docs/03. A Move onto it makes the unit give up where it stands; before the fall it walked to the grove's edge. 30 of 30 seeds felled in random order produce such a hollow. Felling only trees next to reachable ground never does (246 seeds), so M3-2's gather rule decides whether this becomes real. `OrderIntoAFelledGroveMiddle_Report`.
- [S4] BUG-0076: M3-1 nits.
  - docs/03 worst-case setup: measured +2.2 s, docs say +1 s.
  - The full-map flood fill after the ring test can't fail; it is most of that setup time.
  - `{ "resources": [] }` loads clean and then places nothing.
  - -0 mine spacing is accepted (harmless).

Notes (not bugs):
- Large forests are often skipped, as documented: 37 / 384 forests of 200-256 trees on 128 maps, 18-25 / 64 on 1024 maps.
- 4 x 4 mines on 16 x 16 maps: 3 placed over 40 seeds; the oracle holds.
- A `resource-capacity 1000000` replay is accepted and refuses at the first checkpoint within 15 ms.

## Stress results
- Placement oracle: 246 seeds / 6 sets plus 60 hand-map seeds, all pass. Slowest 128-map world (terrain + caps placement): 142 ms. 1024 map at the caps: 2.44-2.63 s (terrain 0.26-0.30 s), limit 5 s.
- Felling oracle: every node of every seed felled in reachable order with random bites. Reach is kept, the grid is restored exactly, and Version = start + nodes. In any order: hollows on 30 / 30 seeds (BUG-0075).
- Depletion vs movement: 100 trees in one tick with 32 cached fields gives a longest wait of 15 ticks (cap 2 builds per tick), 0 give-ups, 32 / 32 arrived. One tree per tick: see BUG-0073.
- Replay format 3: 18 header extremes (int.MaxValue, MinValue, 2^31, 0, 65, 257, min > max, ±Inf, -1, -0) are refused, diverge or play, and never throw. Every-byte truncation of a forests replay is never accepted.
- Capacity: 0 and -5 throw `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` at construction. 1, 12, 13, 14 and 23 fill without a partial forest. 100k with 3,971 nodes: hash 18 µs, 0-byte tick.
