## Findings
- [S2] BUG-0018: More than 32 live move goals rebuilds a flow field for every unit, every tick.
  - Repro: 500 units with goals `targets[i % 33]` cost 322 ms/tick (464 builds per tick); with 64 targets, 359 ms/tick (500 builds). With 32 targets the tick is 0.07 ms. The docs/03 budget is under 4 ms on average.
  - Cause: LRU round-robin worst case, because MovementSystem fetches the field per unit in slot order.
- [S3] BUG-0019: Every Move to a blocked cell runs a full-map `NearestPassable` scan.
  - Repro: 500 Moves to the corner ring cell make the applying tick take 63 ms on the 128 map and 969 ms on a 512 map (Debug).
- [S3] BUG-0020: Arrival is a straight-line check, so a unit "arrives" across a blocked corner.
  - Repro: seed 0, cells (63,61) and (64,62) touch only at a corner. A unit at (127.7,123.7) ordered to (128.3,124.3) goes Idle at once, though the goal is 25.9 cells away by path.
- [S4] (no file) Production diff is about 606 lines against a budget of about 500.
- [S4] (no file) A 1024x1024 World allocates 221 MB and takes 230 ms to construct, mostly the 32 preallocated fields. docs/03 already flags that capacity may need to scale with map size.
