Notes (not bugs; outside documented input or disclosed by the dev):
- Ramp ends are visually steeper than the 30-degree nav limit (about 31 degrees vs 22 degrees mid-ramp), because heights are stored at cell centres. The developer disclosed this; it's visual only.
- A ramp cell whose two axis neighbours don't straddle it (a ramp at the map edge, between two higher cells, or between two ramps of the same height) renders flat at mid-height with walls. A ramp next to a cell two levels up renders as an 8 m slope over one cell. The generator produces neither: all ramps on seeds 1/6/17/99 are sloped, and an EdgeMargin of level-0 ground means no ramps at the edges.
- There are no wall skirts on the map border. That's invisible on generated maps (level-0 margin) but would show on hand-made maps with high ground at the edge.
- 1024x1024: 256 ms, 4.2 M vertices, 904 MiB allocated transiently (List growth), 184 MiB kept. That's outside the documented 128x128 size, so this is a report only.
- At speed 8 below about 32 fps the 5-tick cap is hit every frame, so the effective speed is below 8x. This follows the cap rule by design.

## Stress results
- FixedStepClock fuzz: 5 seeds x 10,000 frames (deltas 0-50 ms, 10% stalls up to 2 s, speeds 0.25-8). Tick count equals the independent cap-rule model on every frame, and alpha matches the model's owed time within 1e-6 s and stays in [0, 1). One hour at 30/60/144/240 fps x speeds 0.25/1/2/8: total ticks within 1 of floor(scaled time / 50 ms). 600,000 frames at exactly 1/60: a tick on every third frame, never drifting. NaN, +/-Inf, Inf x 0, and the smallest double add no ticks and leave alpha unchanged. Stalls of 1e6 s and 1e300 s give 5 ticks, then at most 1 on the next normal frame. 100 back-to-back 2 s stalls give 5 each.
- TerrainMeshBuilder: 19 adversarial maps (1x1 at level 0, level 2 and ramp; 1x9; 9x1; 1x9 alternating ramps; uniform 16x16; checkerboards 0/2, 0/1 and ramp/plateau; ramps on all four edges and the corners; a ramp straddling both axes; a ramp between two higher cells; a 10-cell ramp chain; 0/1/2 stripes), 200 random valid heightmaps (1-23 per side) and 4 generated maps. All pass the closed-surface checker (indices in range and only within their own quad, no NaN, unit normals, winding matches the normal, one top per cell, every height gap walled exactly, no wall on flush edges). Generated maps: 0 flat ramp cells.
