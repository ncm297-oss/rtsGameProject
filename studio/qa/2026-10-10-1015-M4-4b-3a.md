## Findings
- [S3] BUG-0420: `whenBelowHp` counts a unit that is exactly at the line as below for many decimal fractions. The line is computed in single precision, and `(float)0.15 * 100` is 15.000001, so `IsBelow(15, 100, 0.15f)` returns true. QA found 107 such cases among three-decimal fractions and max hp 1-400. The shipped 0.5 is not affected. Repro: un-skip `QA/PassiveQaTests.ExactlyAtTheLine_IsNeverBelow_ForDecimalFractions`.
- [S3] BUG-0421: hp techs never raise unit hit points, so with Dryjhna's Prophecy researched the Zealot frenzies at 41/63, which is 65 % of the hp it can actually have. Repro: `QA/PassiveQaTests.ProphecyResearchedMidFight_*`; also, `TechStat.Hp` is read only by `PassiveSystem.MaxHp`. This gap existed before the task, and the developer disclosed it, but no bug had been filed.
- [S4] BUG-0422, three nits:
  - docs/03 line 2569 has the mojibake `Âµs`.
  - A Zealot trained in phase 3 of the same tick is missed by Martyrdom. This is documented and consistent with splash and ability resolves.
  - The memory bound gained 58 KB of margin beyond the measured +4,144 bytes; it restores the 72 KB policy rather than keeping the 18.9 KB margin it had.
- Note, not a bug: a stronger temporary Frenzied merged into an entry that the passive keeps alive stays at that strength indefinitely. This follows docs/02's stacking rule, is disclosed in docs/03 "Known limits (1)", and the shipped magnitudes are equal.
- Note, not a bug: a selfAura `UseAbility` still needs an on-map point. This is documented, and the view should send the caster's position.
