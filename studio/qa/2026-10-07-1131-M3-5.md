An effect that matches no unit loads (`{attackType: magic, siege: true}`). A tech id may equal a building id, which makes `requires` ambiguous. The bug also carries an M4 note: TechBonus has no tower/building path for "Ranged Weapons ... and towers".

It is never skipped or lost: once room opens, the unit trains its full time and the tech then runs its full 1,200 ticks. This follows the documented M3-4 rule ("a full cap pauses the queue"). A tech at the head still researches at full pop, and cancelling the blocked head starts the tech in the same tick. If the Producer wants a tech to jump a blocked unit, that is a design change, not a fix.
