## Findings
- [S2] BUG-0740: `[keys]` read-back rejects bindings the Keybinds tab makes (an action taking a later action's default after that one moved, or any swap). The player's keys are lost on the next launch. Repro: `QaM6V8Test`, "round trip of attack-move=H, hold=J". Cause: `KeyMap.Read` checks each key against the defaults of actions it hasn't read yet. The dev's SettingsTest row "two keys swapped" asserts this loss as intended.
- [S3] BUG-0741: the clash rule lets a control-group digit share a lower grid key (card_5 / card_10 → 1 accepted). With the Town Hall selected, 1 then goes to the production card and recalls nothing. Bound to card_14 it would cancel a selected site instead. Repro: `QaM6V8Test`, "group_1 shares '1' with card_5".
- [S3] BUG-0742: `order_stop="Shift+Q"` (or `"Ctrl+1"`) in `[keys]` reads with 0 problems as a masked key (0x2000051). After Apply, no key press fires Stop, and nothing is logged. `TrySet` also accepts `Key.Special`, which doesn't round-trip (code only, not reachable from the tab).
- [S4] BUG-0743: nits.
  - The screen's bottom Defaults button now also resets and saves the keybinds at once.
  - The key buttons on the Keybinds tab are hard to see (flat dark on dark), and the tab stacks two "Default..." buttons.
  - The last-seen amount is sampled per rendered frame, not per tick.
- Notes, not bugs:
  - A lowercase name in the file ("k") reads as K. Fine.
  - Patrol (P) isn't rebindable because it isn't built yet.
  - Modifier-only keys are refused, both on the tab and in the file.
  - CapsLock and similar keys are accepted.
