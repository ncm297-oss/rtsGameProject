## Findings
- [S4] BUG-0520: cast-point rings and resolve flashes are still flat at their centre's height across a cliff. Repro: `AbilityViews.cs` ~465 and ~532 place them at `TerrainHeight.At(centre) + Lift`. The dev proposed this; it is out of the brief's scope and noted in docs/03 "Not yet".
- [S4] BUG-0521: three M4-VH3 nits, none reachable today:
  - `GroundDisc.Place` compares the raw `hole` with the clamped `Hole`, so a ring under 0.22 m would rebuild every frame.
  - `SeenResources` drops an old remembered node unseen when only the reused slot's new footprint is seen.
  - `CombatViews.MaxHp ??=` keeps an earlier `Bind`'s array on a re-bind.
- Notes (not bugs):
  - `GroundPatch.Collect` with inner > outer keeps a few cells, as documented; no caller passes it.
  - `MaxCells` clamps at 4,096 m, so a pathological radius would make `GroundDisc` allocate a buffer of about 16.8 M quads. Shipped radii are at most 16 m.
  - The disc shader has no fog include, so an enemy storm over unexplored ground draws bright on black. This predates M4-VH3 and is the M6 fog-look item in docs/03.
  - BUG-0250 item 2's "0.26 m with a flat 5 cm" evidence was not re-measured: it needs a production edit.
- Bug statuses applied by QA:
  - Fixed: BUG-0430, BUG-0431, BUG-0490, BUG-0400 items 2 and 3, BUG-0126 item 5, BUG-0462 item 1.
  - Wontfix with the reason recorded: BUG-0462 item 3 (M6 art), BUG-0126 item 6 (M6 HUD), BUG-0148 item 1 (M6 props), BUG-0250 item 2 (the reason is in the code comment).
  - README rows updated, and rows added for BUG-0520 and BUG-0521.
- Diff scope: inside the view track (`game/**` outside `game/data`, read-only `sim/Rts.Sim/ViewApi/**` additions, view tests in `sim/Rts.Sim.Tests/ViewApi/` as in earlier view tasks). `TerrainHeight.cs` changed in a comment only.
  - `GroundPatch` / `GroundQuad` read only the immutable `Heightmap` and are allocation-free.
  - Hash twins: the dev's `GroundPatchTests.CollectEveryTick_*` (2 seeds x 600 ticks) and QA's `SeenResources_WithSlotReuse_*` (2 seeds x 1,200 ticks, fog on and off, with reuse) are equal every tick.
