Notes, not bugs (for the Producer):
- **A miss does not use up the first shot.** The reveal (M4-5) fires on a hit, not on a landing that hits nothing. A hidden shooter whose shot misses stays hidden, and its next hit still gets +50 %. This is pinned as today's behaviour in `AMissedShot_DoesNotReveal_SoTheNextHitStillCarriesTheBonus`. docs/02 says "from the moment its attack lands". Decide whether a miss should reveal.
- **A long ability stealth re-arms the bonus.** A timed ability stealth longer than the 3 s reveal comes back after each reveal. So a unit whose cooldown is 3 s or more (the Sapper) gets the bonus on every shot while the ability lasts. This follows docs/02 (Revealed overrides only for its 3 s) and is pinned in the lob scene. The Shadow Archer (2.0 s cooldown, stand-still stealth) is not affected.
- **Q1 (melee splash) has a corner.** The splash factor is read in phase 11 and the direct blow's in phase 10. If the striker is hurt, or a pack mate dies, earlier in the same phase 11, the splash can differ from the blow. No shipped melee splash exists.
