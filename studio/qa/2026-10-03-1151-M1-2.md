## Findings
- [S3] BUG-0007: huge numbers become float Infinity or a negative int in GameData. Setting crossbowman `speed: 1e300` loads OK with `SpeedPerTick = ∞` (same for sight, range, splash, bonusVs, multipliers, gather rates). `popCap: 2000000000` gives `HalfPopCap = -294967296`. A related S4 in the same file: 1e10 s gives the misleading message "rounds to -2147483648 ticks".
- [S3] BUG-0008: duplicate JSON keys are silently taken last-wins. `"hp": 55, "hp": 5500` loads OK with Hp 5500, and a duplicate `bonusVs` key does the same.
- [S4] BUG-0009: `requires: [null]` and `tags: ["", null]` are copied into `ImmutableArray<string>` with no error.
- [S4] BUG-0010: a faction with `{"units": []}`, or two units in one slot, loads clean. docs/02 says every faction fills all 7 slots. The developer had already flagged this. Related unchecked rules: minRange > range, windup > cooldown.
- Not a bug, a note: durations that aren't a multiple of 50 ms round silently (the developer flagged this too).
