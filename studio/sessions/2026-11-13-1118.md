## Decisions and surprises
- **`QaM6V20Test`, QA's new scene, is red on the merged tree and lands red by ruling (rule 57, BUG-1323, S3, filed by
  the Producer).** Its restart row fails when the object count rises on three restarts running: 3638, 3648, 3688, 3708
  in the Producer's loop and in 4 of 5 reruns. That count includes wrappers waiting for the .NET garbage collector; the
  same scene run for 16 restarts on a scratch copy saws between 3,623 and 3,708 and never trends, and nodes, resources
  and orphans are exactly flat. A test defect, not a leak. Holding two models and the import tooling a session for a
  few lines in a test was the worse choice; the fix is the first item of the view's next brief and every loop until
  then expects 100 of 101.
- **BUG-1321 stays S3 (Producer ruling).** The severity table reads "perf budget blown" as S2, but the brief made the
  cost a measurement (rule 48) and optimisation out of scope, two of thirty-odd unit types are models, a real match
  fields far fewer than 400 of those two, and `--placeholder-art` is the way back. The price of the ruling is rule 60:
  no further model type lands until the 400-figure frame is under 16.7 ms measured alone; it is the first item of the
  view's hardening next session.
- **The Crossbowman's team part includes the jerkin** (the hood and pauldron alone were 2-3 pixels at the default zoom).
  Accepted: the brief allowed another part on a screenshot's evidence. Producer default, owner may revisit.
- **No crossbow, bow or tool clip exists in the two animation libraries' Standard editions:** both types attack with a
  one-armed shot, gold is gathered with a harvesting motion, the Laborer carries no tool. Accepted for a first slice.
- **The view task was about twice the size asked** (about 1,580 lines against 800: tooling 700, view 400, scenes 670).
  Accepted (inside `max_task_lines` without the QA scenes, rule 39); the next art brief splits tooling from view code.
- **At the default zoom the figures read as thin beside the capsules.** A real person is 0.5 m wide where the capsule
  is 0.8 m. Left as the owner's taste call (STATE, Waiting on you): write "models: bigger".
- **The sim's fix broke on the rows it was written for** (BUG-1310 / 1311): rule 59.
- **The loader's `RawValue`** (so an explicit JSON `null` is an error) is an intentional exception to "absent = null",
  stated in docs/03. Accepted.
- **Provisional opening for D31: V24 with its own `reserveArmyShare` 0.5** (6 of 8 seeds meet BUG-1241's target, against
  3 of 8 today, with fewer defeats than V14's 26-27). The 0.5 was measured through the global field (it moved the
  opponent's gate too): the data's clean-up re-measures it per opening before the landing.
- **New Request 53 (sim):** the Normal AI almost never casts Blood-oil Frenzy; `ai.json` can only field more Shamans, the

- **The data's hardening comes before the landing** (counter 4 / 4): its debt is exactly what the landing needs
