### Decisions and surprises
- **Producer decision (lead rule, docs/01 (g)):** accepted. The docs/02 rule as written cannot give "slow units almost
  never dodge" at any projectile speed or tolerance (the dev's `HitRateByDistance_Report` shows the gap); a rule keyed on
  the target's speed is the smallest fix that keeps every number in data (`leadSpeed`), adds no stored state, and
  costs 0.015 ms a tick at 1,000 shots. Cost: slow units never dodge by walking (the doc said "almost never"); bolts bend
  slightly for a turning walker (the view interpolates). The owner may prefer a flight-time-scaled tolerance or no lead
  (listed under For your review with the alternatives). docs/02 "Projectiles" rewritten so the sentence says the rule
  rather than qualifying the old one (QA's note).
- The `ProjectileCapacity` default (shooters by pop cap, not unit slots) was the dev's deviation for the 1,024-map memory
  bound (`FieldBuildFairnessQaTests`, 228 MB, 33 KB headroom; the default costs 17 KB there, 8 KB headroom left). Accepted;
  the next sim hardening should re-baseline that bound with a reason rather than let the next feature trip it.
- The dev observed walkers zig-zag onto 2 m cell lanes / 45° turns (flow-field legs), which is why "lead at firing only"
  missed 6-10 % at 14-16 m. A movement polish note for the crowd-cost work after M4, not a bug.
- The sim track's fourth feature session is next; then its hardening session (BUG-0144, 0149, 0157, 0151, 0134, the
  memory-bound re-baseline, BUG-0184, BUG-0158).
