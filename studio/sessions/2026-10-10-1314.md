### What changed
- **Hit points and hp techs (BUG-0421).** `UnitStore.TrySpawn` starts every unit at `World.MaxHpOf(owner, type)` (the type's
  `hp` + the owner's `hp` tech bonus, floor 1); `ProductionSystem.Researched` adds the max change to every live unit of the
  player whose max the tech changes (`TechState.Adds` gives the tech's own share, so nothing is stored; floor 1; a dead unit
  untouched). `World.MaxHp(slot)` is the read for hp bars. docs/02 one sentence; docs/01 row.
- **The exact line (BUG-0420).** `UnitPassive.HpLine` in whole millionths (set whenever `HpFraction` is), `PassiveSystem.IsBelow`
  a 64-bit integer compare; 0.15 x 100 = 15 is not below, 14 is. The content hash still hashes the float: unchanged.
- **Summons.** `AbilityKind.Summon` = 2 cast like a `selfAura` (`AbilitySystem.AtCaster`: no range, the point ignored, never
  walks); `AbilityEffectKind.Spawn` = 3 (`unit` own faction, `count` 1-8 = `DataLimits.MaxSummonCount`, `lifetime` ≥ 1 tick;
  errors at `abilities[k].effects[j].<field>`; refused on other kinds; a summon needs a spawn). `SummonSystem.Summon` at the
  resolve after the other effects: `FreeCellSearch.NearestWithin` 3 rings (6 m) round the caster's cell, file / count order,
  the flow-field builder's idle scratch, a full store or no cell drops the rest, spatial hash rebuilt once; the unit has full
  hp incl. techs, no cost, no pop, `ExpiresAtTick` = resolve + lifetime. `SummonSystem.Run` first in phase 5 (returns at 0
  `SummonedUnits`): `RecordDeath(DeathEvent{Expired: true, killer -1})` (no kill / loss, no `onDeath`), then `Free`. Hash: bit
  19 + the end tick for summoned units only; the flag and count derived. `ContentHash` adds spawn fields on spawn effects only:
  the shipped `data-hash` stays D22C849A175F9ED5. `targetUnit` / `autocast` deferred (Producer decision). ~2,990 lines incl. QA.
