### What changed
- **The flank rule** (`MovementSystem`, plan pass): a walker that made no progress, whose owner has a planted unit (`PlantedOwners`, one
  bit pass per plan pass, only once a walker is stuck) and whose way is blocked by an own `Attacking` / `Casting` unit (`OwnPlantedInWay`:
  the neighbours Plan already holds, no query) finds the **block** (`FlankBlockOf`: own planted units linked by gaps under its diameter,
  cached per `PlugEpoch` and radius class, at most `MaxPlugCluster` 32: a bigger block is an army's front, no flank), measures its two
  edges (`FlankTurn`, the detour intervals chained) and turns toward the nearer end by up to `MaxFlankTurn` 135° on open ground
  (`DetourTurn` with a turn bound and a side preference). Strikers on the walker's own target **building** within its reach are left out
  (slice 1's spots); enemy units are never in a block (BUG-0045 holds). The step holds the stuck count except one tick in a stride that
  grows with the line (`FlankCountStride` 4, `FlankMembersPerStride` 2).
- **The flank mark** (`UnitStore.Flank`, fix round 1): (tick + 1) × 128 + members × 2 + side; a flank round ≥ `FlankKeepMinMembers` 11
  is remembered `FlankMemoryTicks` 20 + 3 per member: the walker keeps its side (up to `MaxFlankKeptTurn` 180°), measures the line
  against the way to its goal, and its walk back counts by the stride. **Hashed (bit 20 + the mark) once non-zero**: a match with no
  flank hashes as before (golden unmoved).
- **No give-up while flanking** (`CombatSystem.FriendFightsTarget`'s slice-2 tail): a friend fighting the same unit target from any range
  counts when the chaser flanked within the stall window (`Flanking`), stands within `QueueRange` of such a friend (`QueuedBehindOwnLine`,
  one small query on a stalled scan), its chase's goal is a standing point in its reach (`StandsInReachAtGoal`) and its walk reaches it
  (`ReachableOnFoot`). The same `StandsInReachAtGoal` test now guards M4-H3's ordered keep and BUG-0731's spot keep (BUG-0880 / 0882: a
