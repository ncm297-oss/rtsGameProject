### What changed
- **Hard gathers 25 % faster:** each player's gold / wood rate per tick is worked out once when the world is built (`World.GoldPerTickOf` / `WoodPerTickOf`, `GatherBonusOf`: the seat's difficulty block in its faction's `ai.json`); `EconomySystem.Work` reads the owner's rate. Derived, not saved, not hashed; a bonus of 0 is the old rate bit for bit. Keyed on the seat's difficulty, not the controller, so a recording of a Hard AI plays back as it was played.
- **Hard focuses fire:** every think, a Hard seat's wave members (owed nothing) and defenders inside the base pick one focus: the lowest-hp enemy unit seen within some fighter's own reach (its attack range + radius + 2 m, capped at `rules.json` `ai.focusReach` 14 m), army units before workers; every fighter with it in reach gets `Attack` on it unless it already holds that order or gave that unit up (BUG-0595 / 0596's churn guard). `Focused` wave flag: a member whose focus died is re-sent its AttackMove without spending a try. Bucket grid per seat, no allocation per think.
- **Hard pulls back the wounded, once:** a wave member below its faction's `retreat.hpFraction` (0.3) of its max hp is sent home out of the wave (no loss toward the retreat); the next wave takes it along flagged `FightsOn` (bit 0x80 of the saved `WaveFlag` byte, no format change) and it fights that one out. Decimal compare so "below" is exact at the boundary (BUG-1122 a).
