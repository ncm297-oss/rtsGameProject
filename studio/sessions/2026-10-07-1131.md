### Decisions and surprises
- **Interpretations recorded as Producer defaults** (docs/01 + docs/03, owner may revisit): the Forge table's "+1 / +2"
  are totals (each level adds 1); "melee units" = melee attack type, so **workers get Melee Weapons**; "non-siege" = not

  must be its own; `CancelTrain` cancels techs too (no new kind); `CanResearch` ignores `requires` until M3-6.

  golden's checkpoints did not move. Accepted (same pattern as M3-4's production bits).
- Process: the dev killed four `python` Store-stub processes it had started; QA stopped two of its own. Both named them
  by command line and start time. The rule stands: only processes you started.
