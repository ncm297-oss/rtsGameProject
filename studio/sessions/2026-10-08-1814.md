### What changed
- **Fog:** per player a row-major byte per cell (0 unexplored / 1 explored / 2 visible) plus packed explored bits; rebuilt in
  phase 12 on ticks where `tick % 4 == 1` (all players the same tick; builder's choice) and once at tick 0 before the first
  commands. Each live own unit (any state) and own building (sites too) stamps a precomputed circle (one per distinct radius,
  squared integer cell distances, no trig) as row spans into a per-level layer over the flow-field build scratch; a combine
  pass applies the high-ground rule per cell (a layer sees its own level and below; the 4 m lip goes into the top layer) and
  turns last update's visible cells explored. Blob units sharing a cell skip repeated stamps (a mark in the byte's high bits).
- **Data:** `BuildingDef.Sight` from an optional `sight` (> 0, ≤ `DataLimits.MaxSight` 64) else `rules.json` `buildingSight`
  (required; shipped 12); both watch towers `"sight": 24`; both in `ContentHash`; unit `sight` validated the same way.
- **Targeting:** `VisionSystem.UnitSeesUnit / UnitSeesBuilding` = the unit's own sight right now (with the level rule) or its
  owner's fog visible or a reveal. Asked by the scan (only of a candidate that would win, BUG-0217), the building scan,
  retaliation, `MayAttack` (an explicit Attack on an unseen target is dropped) and a kept target on scan ticks (lost → given up
  by the BUG-0137 rule or disengaged). On a one-level map every candidate within sight is seen by the scanner itself, so flat
  fights play exactly as before (counter-triangle output byte-identical, QA).
- **Reveal:** `PendingHit.AttackerLevel` (the melee striker's cell level, or `ProjectileStore.Level` recorded at `Fire`); a hit
  from above reveals the attacker to the victim's owner for 40 ticks (per unit slot x player: until-tick + generation, so a
  recycled slot never inherits one). Hashed: explored bits, live reveals, the projectile level byte. Derived: visible bits,
  `Version`.
- **View surface:** `World.Fog.Visibility(player)`, `Version(player)`, `IsVisible`, `IsExplored`, `CanSeeUnit`, `CanSeeBuilding`

### Decisions and surprises
- **Producer decision (BUG-0215, reverses the brief's "visible bits derived"; owner may revisit):** the visible bits are state
  (they depend on where units stood at the last update, which positions no longer hold), so the sim's hardening session
  **hashes them too**, packed (one `ulong` per 64 cells per player), and docs/03's save sentence changes to "the save stores
  them". Cost ≤ ~20 µs a tick; golden regen once with the proof. Alternative: save only on update ticks (fragile).
- The builder's "combat also counts the unit's own sight at this tick" keeps every flat fight identical and makes the gate
  cheap on one-level maps; the price is that the view (last update's fog) can lag combat by up to 4 ticks (BUG-0216 item 3):
  M4-V4 should expect a unit to shoot a target the screen still hides for 0.2 s.
- Three tracks' suites at once: `TightBlob2500` fails on base and head alike (4.73-5.09 ms); counts only alone (BUG-0158).
- Lesson kept: a sim task that changes unit behaviour runs the scene loop against the **merged** view branch, not only its
  own `game/tests` (BUG-0218 appeared only on the merge).
