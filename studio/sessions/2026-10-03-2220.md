## What changed
- Ramp walls (BUG-0011): `NavGrid.IsRampWall` marks the flat lower cells flanking a ramp along its
  length as `Cliff | Blocked`, so a ramp is a corridor entered only at its mouth and foot and no
  passable 4-neighbour step exceeds 30°. `MapAssert` now checks the rise rule too.
- Param safety (BUG-0012): `MapGenParams.Validate` bounds `EdgeMargin`/`RampWidth`/`RampLength`/
  `Level2Inset` at the smaller map side before any arithmetic; `Heightmap` uses `long` cell math.
- Generation time (BUG-0013, then BUG-0015): caps `MaxRampTries` 128, `MaxMaxAttempts` 8,
  `MaxPlateaus` 32; ramp tries are O(1)+O(ramps)+O(width) via a summed-area table and rectangle
  overlap (maps bit-identical to before, pinned by 15 hashes and QA oracles); NavGrid ctor fast
  path and bounds-free Flood. Worst valid 1024x1024 case: 1.3 s Debug / 0.3 s Release.
- `Rts.Sim.Spatial.SpatialHash` (223 lines): 4 m buckets (2 cells, derived from `MapConstants`),
  counting sort into flat arrays, zero allocation; `QueryRadius`, `QueryRect`, `NearestEnemy`
  (any other owner = enemy; ties -> lowest slot); results ascending by slot; return value = total
  matches (above buffer length = truncated); out-of-map units clamp to edge buckets. Owned by
  `World.Spatial`, rebuilt in `Simulation.Tick()` right after commands apply. Not in `StateHash`.
- docs/03: tick phase 1 text, "Spatial hash" Implementation (M1-4a), nav grid ramp-wall sentence,
  generator caps/timing paragraph.
