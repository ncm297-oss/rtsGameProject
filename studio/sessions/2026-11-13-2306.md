## Decisions and surprises
- **BUG-1321 is closed for 400 figures and rule 60 is lifted.** Measured alone at this ACCEPT: 10.1 ms with the whole
  crowd alive against a 16.7 ms limit (it was 14.5-21). The honest column is "while 90%+ alive": the whole-run average
  falls as units die. The worst 1% of frames sits at 15.6-15.9 ms, just inside a frame.
- **1,000 animated figures are still 23-27 ms** (about 10,500 draw calls). Not a bug today (two unit types are models
  and no match fields 1,000 of them), but it is the wall the art pass meets when every type is a model: before the
  last third of the roster is imported, a task measures a 1,000-figure mixed crowd and decides on merged surfaces, a
  shared skeleton or baked animation. Recorded in the view's debt list.
- **Request 53 changed owner.** The AI does not cast Frenzy because it trains no Shaman before 15:00, which is the
  opening's composition (`ai.json`, the data track). Whether a Shaman then keeps up with the army, and whether the cast
  rule suits a buff, is still unmeasured (BUG-1340): the sim re-measures after the data fields Shamans. No rule is
  built on a guess.
- **The sim developer skipped the full loop after its last fix** (an `int?` changed to `int` for one red row); QA's
  full run and the Producer's merged run cover it. Second time in two sessions (M6-V20's developer did the same): the
  next briefs say "the full default loop after the last edit, or say that you did not".
- **The data developer edited two of QA's tests** because the change required it (the landing copy now differs from
  shipped in one more field; the Owner review table's row count). Inside the data track's files, read at ACCEPT, sound.
- **A status is "a faction's own" when its id starts `<faction>_`** (new content-test convention, the data track's):
  accepted; it matches docs/03's naming for `teblor_frenzied`.
- **The reserve share for D31 is picked at the PLAN, and the choice is between own 0.25 and own 0.5.** Both meet the
  target on 6 of 8 seeds. 0.25 keeps a larger army at 15:00 (56 v 36 pop) and builds every building kind in 8 matches
  (0 at 0.5); 0.5 is the value the landing copy, QA's rehearsal and the sim's report already carry. The Gatherer's
  `carry` 15 moves the Teblor economy too, so D31 re-runs the chosen row with it before anything ships (rule 41).
- **The landing's expected reds are 37, not 24** (QA's rehearsal with V24 swapped in): the 13 more are AI-page and
  opening pins the landing rewrites; nothing sim-owned beyond the two golden hash pins.
- **"Total LFS under 12 MB" was a loose criterion** (the Producer's): the repo's LFS is 34.5 MB with the look test.
  Future briefs say "the art pass's binaries".
- BUG-1350 (S3) is a rare ERROR line under load in a QA scene, not a red row on purpose: rule 57 does not apply; it is
