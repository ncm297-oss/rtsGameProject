### What changed
- **Cooldown techs:** the cooldown an ability starts is `max(1, CooldownTicks + round(TechBonus(owner, type,
  AbilityCooldown)))`, read at the resolve, so Moranth Supply makes the Cusser 30 s instead of 45 s; a tech landing while a
  cooldown runs changes the next one, not that one (docs/03).
- **Loader rule:** a `damageOverTime` status's `duration` must be a whole number of seconds, at least 1 (a `DataError` at
  the field); slows keep any duration of at least a tick. Two extra rules: `friendlyFire` > 0 only with `enemy_units`
  (the other `affects` already take own units in full); `buildings` refused with `own_units`.
- **The Cusser:** `damage` effects take `buildings` (other players' buildings whose footprint rectangle is within the
  radius, as `structure`, through `CombatSystem.HitBuilding`; never own buildings) and `friendlyFire` 0-1 (own units in
  the radius, the caster included, scaled like splash); no falloff. `cusser` in `malazan/abilities.json` (6 m / 3.5 m /
  1.0 s / 45 s / siege 120 / `enemy_units`), the Sapper's `abilities`. `ContentHash` covers the new fields; golden
  `data-hash` 7E04011FC88881F3 → 41842085985611BF (every `k` line equal; added to `SameGameDataHashes`).
- Docs/03 "Implementation (M4-4b-1)", "Data format", "For the data track"; docs/01 row; docs/02 one sentence under
  "Ability system".
