### What changed
- **Pocket rule** (`NavGrid.ClearFootprint`, `NavFlags.Pocket` = 32): a freed footprint (Cancel, `Damage` to 0, a node
  depleted) and the pocket cells 4-connected to it reopen together (one `Version` bump) only if the union touches a
  passable cell; otherwise the cells stay `Blocked | Pocket` with nothing published. The flood borrows
  `FlowFieldCache.BuildScratch` (`NavGrid.ShareScratch`), so it allocates nothing. Invariant: every passable cell
  reaches every other after any sequence of placements, cancels, destruction and felling. Closes BUG-0093.
- **Cheap-first Build** (`ConstructionSystem.Check(..., worker, sealLast)`): the apply path runs UnknownType to
  StoreFull before the seal flood; `CanPlace` keeps the documented order; pass / fail identical (QA: 1,190 probes).
  100 refused Builds at a long-detour anchor: 22 ms → 0.07 ms. The issuing worker's own Hold never blocks its Build
  (the one documented CanPlace / Build divergence). Closes BUG-0091 and BUG-0092 (b).
- **Push-out**: rings go on outward until a free cell (no stacking); occupants through the spatial hash (rebuilt
  once per push-out) with per-cell answers in the build scratch; `PrevPosition` set with `Position`. 400 units round
  a Keep: 7.3 ms → 0.3 ms. `EconomyConstants.PushRings` removed. Closes BUG-0092 (c)-(e).
