  and the one behind ~1.8 m, out of `Reach` (1.25 m); when the front left, the next front "arrived" against the queue
  behind it on every 20-tick retry, for the rest of the match. Three changes for footprint walkers only:
  `MovementSystem.QueuesBehind` (a touched arrived groupmate stops the walker only if it stands nearer the goal),
