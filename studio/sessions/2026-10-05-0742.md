## What changed
- `MovementSystem` now plans every Moving unit from start-of-tick state, then applies (two passes),
  so unit-unit interaction is symmetric and walk order can't change results. Per unit: one
  `Spatial.QueryRadius` (own radius + data max radius + 1 m `AvoidRange`), overlap-weighted push
  (half vs walkers, all vs arrived groupmates), "keep right" sidestep around standing/oncoming units,
  standing non-groupmates as hard walls (collide-and-slide), refused steps slide along the wall.
- Crowded arrival: arrive within 1 m of the point or when touching an arrived groupmate (same
  `GoalCell`, Idle), but only if no neighbor is closer than 0.6 x radii sum; else back off. Arrived
  units keep `GoalCell` and anchor the blob.
- Give-up: `UnitStore.StuckTicks` / `BestRemaining` (both hashed); progress = beating the best
  path-left estimate by 0.25 x speed; 20 stuck ticks -> Idle with `GoalCell = -1`. Back-off ticks
  count too; at the limit a backing-off unit stops where it is and keeps its goal (BUG-0027).
- `Move` to the goal cell a unit already holds is the same order: Idle stays put, Moving takes the
  new point but keeps its order tick and stuck count (BUG-0029).
- Adjacent-cell steering: goal one legal 8-neighbor step away -> aim straight at the goal.
- Test seams `World`/`Simulation(SimConfig, Heightmap?)` (internal) for hand-made maps.
- docs/03 "Local movement" rewritten (implementation, known limits), StateHash, tick phase 9.
